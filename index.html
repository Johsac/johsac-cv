AboutAcerca de mí

PhD Researcher in Generative AI & Large Language Models at the Laboratory of Computing and Automation (LCA), Universidade Estadual de Campinas (UNICAMP), Brazil. My research focuses on multimodal generative architectures for 3D gesture synthesis driven by speech and text — winner of the Reproducibility Award at GENEA 2025 (ACM Multimedia).

With a Master's in Statistics (UNICAMP, 2023) and a background in Mechanical Engineering (ESPOL, 2020), I bridge the gap between statistical rigor and AI innovation. My stack spans RAG, LangChain, PyTorch, Agentic AI, and I'm passionate about building production-ready AI systems that serve both academic research and industry applications in Tech, FinTech, and Consulting.
